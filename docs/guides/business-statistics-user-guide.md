# Business statistics: views (user guide)

## For business owners
1. Sign in and open **My business → Settings → Estadísticas** (`/partner/settings/stats`).
2. Choose the period: **last 7, 30 or 90 days**.
3. You see three figures (views in the period, today and the last 7 days) and a chart with a bar per day. Days without views have no bar.

| Situation | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| No business registered yet | A message asking you to register one |
| No views in the period | The figures at 0 and a message |
| The statistics cannot be loaded (e.g. you are not the owner) | An error message |

## Privacy
- Only a **count per day** is stored. Nothing about the people who visit your page: no name, account, IP address or device.
- **Your own visits are not counted.**
- A visitor who reloads the page is counted again, because visitors cannot be told apart without personal data. Days are UTC.

## Good to know
Views are counted when the public page of a business is opened. Until that page shows real businesses (it still shows sample profiles), the numbers stay at 0.
